Subject: Try the Community Bail Research volunteer search

The volunteer search supports live calendar searches and collected workspace
evidence:
https://community-bail-research-tool.replit.app/search

Please ask your workspace coordinator for the shared password through a separate,
trusted channel. The password is intentionally not included in this email.

Choose a supported calendar event and court office, hearing dates, and a maximum
recorded bail amount; then inspect the evidence and download all results or only
records within your amount filter. Live searches cover up to seven days starting
today or later and can pause under source limits or when documents are unavailable.
Collected workspace evidence is also available as a separate mode. Partial searches
remain clearly marked and exportable. Eligible evidence can be explicitly saved
into the shared workspace for review.

A matching amount does not confirm custody, eligibility, the applicable bail
order, or the amount needed for release.

After trying it, please tell us what you actually needed:
https://community-bail-research-tool.replit.app/feedback

What were you trying to do? What got in the way? What would help next?
Feedback is shared within the workspace, so please leave out names, docket
numbers, sensitive case information, passwords and contact details.
