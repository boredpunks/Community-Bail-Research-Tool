The collected-evidence search is ready for volunteer feedback:
https://community-bail-research-tool.replit.app/search

Please ask your workspace coordinator for the shared password through a separate,
trusted channel. The password is intentionally not included in this email.

Choose hearing dates and a maximum recorded bail amount, inspect the evidence,
and download all results or just records within your amount filter. Partial
searches remain clearly marked and exportable. Eligible evidence can be explicitly
saved into the shared workspace for review.

Live calendar discovery is coming soon. This release searches collected dockets,
not all court records. A matching amount does not confirm custody, eligibility,
the applicable bail order, or the amount needed for release.

After trying it, please tell us what you actually needed:
https://community-bail-research-tool.replit.app/feedback

What were you trying to do? What got in the way? What would help next?
Feedback is shared within the workspace, so please leave out names, docket
numbers, sensitive case information, passwords and contact details.
