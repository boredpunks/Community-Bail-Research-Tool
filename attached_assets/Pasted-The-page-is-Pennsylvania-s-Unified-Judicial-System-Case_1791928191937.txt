## 17. The page’s public-record warnings

These warnings are part of the page, not incidental boilerplate. They constrain how research results should be interpreted.

### Updates may be delayed

The portal says recent court-office entries may not immediately appear on docket sheets. It also disclaims liability for inaccurate or delayed data, errors, and omissions.[2]

For a research tool, a search timestamp is not proof that the underlying record was updated at that time.

### This is not a criminal-history background check

The page says docket-sheet information should not replace a criminal-history background check, which can only be provided by the Pennsylvania State Police. It also warns employers about potential civil liability for noncompliance with Pennsylvania’s Criminal History Record Information Act.[2]

That warning concerns appropriate use of the information; it does not turn the search page into a comprehensive criminal-history source.

### Public search is free

The portal says it is not affiliated with fee-charging private public-record search systems and that its web-docket search is a free public service.[2]

### Some records cease to be publicly accessible

The Magisterial District Court note describes a protocol implemented April 1, 2012: when official paper case records have been or should have been destroyed under the applicable retention schedule, the public web docket sheet also becomes inaccessible.[2]

Therefore, absence from the public portal is not, by itself, proof that no case ever existed.

### Philadelphia can have related MC and CP dockets

The page explains that a Philadelphia criminal matter may have both a Municipal Court docket number and a Court of Common Pleas docket number concerning the same incident or offenses.

It directs users to the `cross court docket number` field on the first page of the docket sheet’s case-information section to identify related records.[2]

For research counting, that creates a potential duplication issue: two docket numbers need not represent two unrelated incidents.

### Historical coverage is not necessarily complete

The page references a chart concerning cases migrated from county legacy systems into CPCMS and says not all cases were migrated.[2]

A broad research dataset should therefore distinguish “records found through this portal” from “all historical cases.”
