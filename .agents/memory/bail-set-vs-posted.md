---
name: Bail setting vs. posted security
description: Distinguish a court's bail setting from later cash or surety transactions.
---

When a docket sheet has separate Bail Set and Bail Posted sections, treat them as different facts. A posted cash or surety amount must not replace the bail amount set by the court. Keep the sections, dates, amounts and statuses separate.

**Why:** A source docket showed a later posted-security amount alongside an earlier bail setting; an extractor that scanned both sections selected the wrong dollar value.

**How to apply:** Bound extraction to the relevant source section, bind amounts and percentages to dated rows, and preserve a page quote. A missing positive bail setting is not proof that bail is zero.
